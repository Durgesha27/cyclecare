🌿 Privacy

CycleCare stores only what you give it: your name, email, a hashed password, the period dates and check-ins you record, and your preferences.

  • Records are tied to your account; other users can never open them.
  • Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text.
  • Nothing is sold or shared.
  • Settings lets you download everything as JSON, or permanently delete your account and all related data.
